Two brilliant engineers had built a successful, growing electrical contracting business. The trouble was that the business infrastructure had never caught up with the growth.

## The Starting Point

Turnover was £970,000 and profitability was around 4%. The team was two directors, one administrator and eight engineers.

Work was being assigned to engineers by three different people, using three different methods, which meant daily chaos and inconsistency. Even more concerning was what we found when we looked at the numbers. The directors had estimated their fixed monthly costs at around £6,000 to £7,000. The real figure was £22,000.

Their biggest client, responsible for half a million pounds of annual work, was costing them £10 per job to service. It was actively losing them money.

## A Wake-Up Call

Once the true financial picture emerged, it was a significant wake-up call, and the foundation for a complete strategic reset. Their goals were to overhaul their internal systems and administration, reduce stress, create a business plan, improve time management and work-life balance, and dramatically increase profitability.

## The Work

Over 12 months we held around 70 coaching sessions with both directors and the team, plus 40 check-in sessions. The iCan Thinking mindsets produced a strong shift in thinking across the leadership, and we put the iCan Grow business strategies in place to reshape the business from the ground up:

- **Clear systems.** One way of assigning and managing work, instead of three.
- **Financial visibility.** Knowing the real numbers, every month.
- **A focused growth strategy.** Loss-making clients were released, and profit-generating clients were prioritised and grown.
- **Lower overheads.** New systems removed duplication and slashed costs.

## The Results

Turnover grew to £1,140,000 within 12 months. Profitability rose from 4% to 40%, which generated £456,000 in profit. Both directors were calmer, more strategic and far more confident in their numbers. The business was finally running as efficiently as the engineers themselves.

## Results at a Glance

- **Turnover:** From £970,000 to £1,140,000 in 12 months.
- **Profitability:** From around 4% to 40%.
- **Annual profit:** £456,000.
- **Costs:** The real fixed costs were found, at £22,000 a month, not the £6,000 to £7,000 estimated.
- **Clients:** Loss-making work released, profitable work grown.
- **Stress:** Significantly reduced.

## What This Story Teaches Us

1. **You cannot manage what you cannot see.** The numbers changed every decision.
2. **Not all revenue is good revenue.** Their biggest client was their biggest problem.
3. **Growth needs infrastructure.** Systems let a business grow without chaos.
4. **Confidence comes from clarity.** When the directors knew their numbers, they led with calm.
