Do you struggle to sustain positive change? Most people do. Think of people who join a gym in January. They are super keen for six weeks, then something gets in the way, then activity goes back to little or nothing.

It's the same in business. People watch a video or maybe do a course. They are inspired with good intention, make some changes, but all too quickly return to old habits. So, what is the answer? If you want lasting change you must first change your mindset, then break the habit.

## Mindset first

Mindset is about valuing the new reality more than the old. Until you value the new thing more, the old one wins!

## Then the habit

Habit is about retraining the brain. Your brain learned the old habit and, with practice, you can delete the old one and replace it with a better one.

Easy, right? Of course not, or we'd all be doing it.

## Three keys to sustaining positive change

With the right support, it is easier than you think. The three keys are consistency, repetition, and accountability.

1. **Work out where you are now.** Our Business Analysis tool is a great start.
2. **Build a new "value picture" of the better you.** See it clearly, so that you value it more than the old way.
3. **Be accountable.** Be accountable to yourself, plus someone else who can support and motivate you.
