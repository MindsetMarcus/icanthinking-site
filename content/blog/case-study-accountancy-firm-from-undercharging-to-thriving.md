The principal of this accountancy firm was a wonderfully generous human being. That generosity, lovely as it is, had quietly become her biggest business constraint.

In the early days she had set her fees well below market value, and as the years passed she never recalibrated them. The result was a business doing far more work than it was being paid for. There was no written business plan, her stress levels were high, her hours were excessive and her work-life balance had taken a battering.

## The Starting Point

Turnover was £70,000, profitability was around 10%, and the team was the principal plus one part-time administrator. Her goals were simple to say and hard to do: reduce stress, create a clear business plan, improve her time management, systems and work-life balance, and grow the firm with a significantly healthier profit.

## The Real Work: Permission to Be Paid

The heart of this was a deep mindset piece. We worked on helping her understand her own value, and on giving herself permission to charge accordingly. Until that shifted, no pricing spreadsheet in the world was going to help.

Over 12 months we held around 30 one-to-one coaching sessions and 20 check-in sessions. She used the online portal and worked through the iCan Thinking mindsets, producing a meaningful and lasting shift in how she saw herself and her work.

Alongside the mindset work, we put the iCan Grow business strategies into practice, including a full restructure of her pricing and her approach to marketing.

## The Results

Turnover doubled to £140,000 within 10 months, and profitability jumped from 10% to 40%. She felt noticeably calmer and more engaged, and she reported that her stress levels had fallen by around 90%. She also brought on an additional team member, which significantly improved her work-life balance.

## Results at a Glance

- **Turnover:** Doubled from £70,000 to £140,000 in 10 months.
- **Profitability:** From around 10% to 40%.
- **Stress:** Reported down by around 90%.
- **Team:** An additional team member, giving her time back.
- **Pricing and marketing:** Fully restructured.

## What This Story Teaches Us

1. **Generosity needs boundaries.** Charging fairly is how you keep giving.
2. **Your price is a belief.** Change the belief and the price follows.
3. **Calm is a business asset.** When stress dropped, her decisions and her energy improved.
4. **You do not have to do it all.** The right help, at the right time, gives you your life back.
