Fasting, Autophagy, and the Inner Power to Heal

What your body already knows about preventing disease

  • By Marcus Thomlinson
  • Originally published 16 April 2025
  • 5 min read

In 2016, Japanese cell biologist Yoshinori Ohsumi won the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for discovering one of the body’s most powerful self-healing mechanisms: autophagy.

Autophagy - from the Greek words meaning “self-eating” - is the process by which your cells identify damaged parts, break them down, and recycle them. It’s nature’s built-in system of detox, renewal, and repair.

But what’s truly incredible is this: you can switch this process on through fasting.

In fact, early research suggests that intermittent fasting and time-restricted eating may help support the body’s defences against cancer, Alzheimer’s, Parkinson’s, heart disease, and more - not by adding anything extra to your system, but by creating space for your body to do what it already knows how to do: clean, restore, and renew.

This isn’t just a diet trend. It’s a return to biological wisdom.

What is Autophagy - and Why Does It Matter?

Every day, your cells face stress. Damaged proteins, worn-out organelles, and toxic build-up can accumulate and, if not cleared, can lead to inflammation and degenerative disease.

Autophagy is your internal janitor, mechanic, and recycling centre - all in one. It removes the “junk” before it causes harm and turns waste into usable fuel. Think of it as a kind of deep cellular housekeeping.

Yoshinori Ohsumi’s research showed us that without this process, cells can become dysfunctional - and over time, that dysfunction lays the groundwork for major diseases.

When autophagy is working well, you feel sharper, cleaner, and more energised. When it’s not? Chronic inflammation, brain fog, accelerated ageing, and higher disease risk creep in.

Fasting: The Key to Unlocking Your Body’s Natural Healing

One of the most effective ways to activate autophagy is through fasting - particularly intermittent fasting or extended fasting under professional guidance.

Why?

Because when your body runs low on external nutrients, it becomes more efficient. It doesn’t panic - it cleans house. It switches from growth mode to repair mode. And in doing so, it:

  • Helps clean up misfolded proteins that contribute to diseases like Alzheimer’s

  • Helps target precancerous cells before they can replicate

  • Reduces insulin resistance and systemic inflammation

  • Rejuvenates immune function

Rather than constantly being in a state of digestion, your body shifts into a state of regeneration.

The Link to Alzheimer’s and Neurodegenerative Disease

Alzheimer’s disease, Parkinson’s, and other neurodegenerative conditions are strongly linked to the accumulation of toxic proteins in the brain, such as beta-amyloid plaques.

Autophagy is your brain’s built-in defence against this.

When properly activated, it helps clear out those harmful proteins, and may help protect mental clarity. In fact, studies have shown that impaired autophagy is a common feature of many neurodegenerative diseases.

And again, fasting can help reset and reboot this system.

Cancer: A Cellular Disorder Autophagy Helps Guard Against

Cancer begins when damaged cells stop obeying the rules - they grow, divide, and spread when they shouldn’t. One of the body’s defences is to identify and recycle these rogue cells before they become dangerous.

This is exactly what autophagy does.

In the early stages of tumour development, it acts like an internal watchdog - spotting abnormalities and removing them quietly. While the relationship between autophagy and cancer is complex (in some late-stage tumours, autophagy may help cancer survive), autophagy’s protective role in the early stages is well recognised.

And again - fasting turns that process on.

The Mindset of Renewal: You Are Designed to Heal

This is where mindset meets biology.

Fasting requires a mental shift. In a world of 24/7 access and constant consumption, choosing to pause - to withhold - can feel radical. But it is a deeply empowering act. One that aligns with your body’s natural rhythm.

You don’t need expensive supplements or complicated regimens. You need space. You need intention. And most of all, you need to trust your body.

The real power of fasting isn’t about restriction. It’s about activating renewal.

So, How Do You Start?

Here are simple, research-backed ways to experiment with fasting to support autophagy and disease prevention:

1. Time-Restricted Eating

Start with a 12:12 schedule - 12 hours of eating, 12 hours of fasting (e.g. 8 am to 8 pm). Then slowly move toward 14:10 or 16:8. This gentle approach is sustainable and effective.

2. 24-Hour Fast (Once Weekly or Monthly)

With medical advice, fasting for 24 hours once in a while can deeply activate autophagy and give your system a reset.

3. Autophagy-Boosting Lifestyle Habits

  • Regular exercise (especially HIIT and endurance)

  • Prioritising sleep

  • Reducing processed sugar and refined carbohydrates

  • Managing stress with meditation, nature, or breathwork

The Bigger Picture: Fasting is a Metaphor for Life

Here’s the mindset shift that I invite you to consider:

Just like your body, your life needs regular moments of pause.

Periods where you stop “consuming” - food, information, distractions - and give yourself space to reflect, release, and restore.

Whether it’s emotional baggage, outdated beliefs, or stressful patterns, you need your own version of mental and spiritual autophagy.

Fasting reminds us that we don’t grow by constantly adding more - we grow by removing what no longer serves us.

Final Thought: The Power is Already Inside You

Yoshinori Ohsumi’s Nobel Prize discovery wasn’t just a gift to medicine. It was a gift to all of us - a reminder that your body holds wisdom that modern life often drowns out.

You don’t need to live in fear of disease. You can support the very processes that help protect you, right now, from within.

And the most powerful switch might be something as simple as choosing when not to eat.

You are more intelligent, more adaptive, and more resilient than you think.

Autophagy is your proof. Fasting is your tool. And your mindset is the key.

A quick note: this post is for general information and inspiration. It is not medical advice. Please speak to your GP before trying fasting, especially if you have a medical condition, take medication, are pregnant, or have ever had a difficult relationship with food.
