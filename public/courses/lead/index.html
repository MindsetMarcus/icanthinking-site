Why it matters

The way your leaders think shapes the way your business performs.

Every team reflects its leader. Under pressure, leaders who are stretched and reactive create stress, mixed messages and people who quietly look for the door. Leaders with a calm, clear and positive mindset create the opposite: trust, energy and results. Skills training only goes so far. iCan Lead goes to the root, how leaders think, because mindset is what decides how every skill gets used.

What your company gains

Calmer decisions

Leaders who pause before they react make better calls, even in a crisis.

Engaged, loyal people

Teams who feel heard and trusted stay longer and give more.

A healthier culture

Less blame, less burnout, more ownership and honest conversation.

Confident succession

Develop the leaders you already have, rather than hoping to recruit them.

Questions

Quick answers about iCan Lead.

What is iCan Lead?

It is a leadership development programme for teams, built around the Top 10 Mindsets of Great Leaders. It helps managers and directors lead with clarity, calm and purpose, so their people do their best work.

Who is iCan Lead for?

Managers, directors and senior leaders, and the companies that want to develop them. Individual leaders are welcome too, and entry-level places are available.

How is it different from skills training?

Skills training only goes so far. iCan Lead goes to the root, how leaders think, because mindset decides how every skill gets used.

How long are the modules?

There are ten short modules of about 30 minutes each, so busy leaders can fit them around real work. Every module gives a clear mindset, a practical tool and an action to take straight away.

What does each leader take away?

Mindset Mantras to reset in seconds, the Delete That Programme tool for limiting beliefs, the goal formula SFB = A², a personal Mindset Map, and access to the iCan app and community for habits, goals and ongoing support.

How does it work for a team?

Leaders learn through guided videos and worksheets in the iCan app, practise in a weekly live drop-in with teaching, coaching and accountability, and senior leaders can go deeper with private one-to-one coaching.

How much does it cost?

The course is included with iCan Commit at £19 a month, or £190 a year. The weekly leadership drop-in starts from £297 a month, with a six month minimum and then one month's notice. One-to-one coaching with iCan Personal starts from £695 a month. For company teams, book a free discovery call and we will talk it through.

Are places limited?

Yes. Places for company teams are limited so every leader is heard.

What happens on a discovery call?

It is a free, friendly conversation about your team, your goals and whether iCan Lead is the right fit. No pressure, just a conversation.
