Questions

Quick answers about iCan Be Wealthy.

What is iCan Be Wealthy?

It is a course that helps you untangle your beliefs about money and learn the basics of wealth, investing and financial planning.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is education to help you think more clearly about money. It is not personal financial advice.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. Join iCan Start free for three taster lessons, and try Module 7 of the Foundation Course, which introduces the mindset behind wealth.

How much does it cost?

The course is included with iCan Commit at £19 a month or £190 a year, with a founding member price of £14 a month until 2 January 2027.
