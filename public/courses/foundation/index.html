Questions

Quick answers about iCan Thinking Foundation Course.

What is the iCan Thinking Foundation Course?

It is 21 short, practical modules that teach you to change the way you think so you can change what you can do. It is designed to help you beat stress, work less and be more of a success.

How long does each module take?

Each module is a standalone mini course of about 20 minutes of core learning, starting with a 5 to 7 minute video. The daily actions then take anything from 5 minutes to an hour, and you set the pace.

How is iCan thinking different from positive thinking?

Positive thinking on its own can feel like wishful thinking. iCan thinking adds belief that is backed by action, using See it, Feel it, Believe it and then doing it every day.

Do I need to download anything?

No. The course lives in the iCan app, which runs in any web browser on your phone, tablet or computer.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. Join iCan Start free and you'll get three taster lessons: an overview of iCan Thinking, reprogramming the brain, and finding calm.

How does the weekly drop-in work?

iCan Together gives you a live hour every week: 20 minutes of teaching on your course, 20 minutes of live coaching on questions you send in beforehand, and 20 minutes of accountability. It starts from £97 a month and includes everything in iCan Commit.

How long do I commit for?

Weekly drop-ins are a six month engagement, paid monthly, then one month's notice. That is about how long it takes to put the work in.
