Questions

Quick answers about iCan Grow.

What is iCan Grow?

iCan Grow is a business plan and user manual in one, built step by step in the iCan app. It helps you move from working in your business to working on it.

Who is it for?

Owner-managed businesses of any size, from a one-person start-up to an established firm. Even if you work alone today, a manual makes it easy to bring in your first helper.

Do I need to do the Foundation Course first?

No. If you've done it you'll feel right at home, and if you're new, the introduction gives you the essentials.

What do I end up with?

One single document that is your business plan and your user manual, telling the complete story of your business in your own words.

How does fee deferment work?

For start-ups and businesses going through a tough patch, you can defer between 25% and 75% of the fee until the end of the programme. Terms are agreed in writing on a discovery call.
