The optional success fee

Only on growth you would not have had anyway.

We begin by working out how much your profit has grown on its own, on average, over the last three years. That is your natural growth rate. The success fee is 5% of any profit growth above that, and nothing on growth you would have had anyway.

Questions

Quick answers about iCan Grow.

What is iCan Grow?

iCan Grow is a business plan and user manual in one, built step by step in the iCan app. It helps you move from working in your business to working on it.

Who is it for?

Owner-managed businesses of any size, from a one-person start-up to an established firm. Even if you work alone today, a manual makes it easy to bring in your first helper.

Do I need to do the Foundation Course first?

No. If you've done it you'll feel right at home, and if you're new, the introduction gives you the essentials.

What do I end up with?

One single document that is your business plan and your user manual, telling the complete story of your business in your own words.

How much does the iCan Grow programme cost?

The one-to-one programme is from £12,000 for 12 months. It includes 24 one-to-one coaching and strategy sessions, unlimited focus sessions and messaging, a written summary after every session and your Private Coaching Portal. You can pay in full, spread it over 12 monthly payments, or use Pay As You Grow.

What is Pay As You Grow?

You defer 25%, 50% or 75% of the fee until the end of the 12 months. The deferred balance is only payable if all your agreed targets have been hit, including the money being in the bank. If they are not achieved, it does not become due. The 50% option is the most popular, for example 12 monthly payments of £500 followed by £6,000 at the end.

What is the optional success fee?

It is 5% of your profit growth above your natural growth rate, which we work out from the average of your last three years. For example, if you would normally grow profit by £10,000 a year and you grow it by £20,000, the fee is 5% of the extra £10,000, which is £500.

What is iCan Mastermind?

It is a business group of up to 12 owner-managed businesses at £297 a month, on a 12 month engagement. You get a weekly mastermind session, guest speakers, shared goal boards, strategic check-ins with a coach and access to the Foundation and iCan Grow courses.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Every option can be spread over 12 monthly payments. Terms are agreed in writing on a discovery call.
