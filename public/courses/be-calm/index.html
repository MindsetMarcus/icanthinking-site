Questions

Quick answers about iCan Be Calm.

What is iCan Be Calm?

It is a course on how to understand, manage and overcome stress, with practical tools you can use straight away, built into the iCan app.

Who is it for?

Anyone who feels stressed, overwhelmed or on edge, from busy professionals and business owners to parents and anyone who wants a calmer life.

Is it therapy?

No. iCan Be Calm is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment. If stress is affecting your health, please speak to your GP, and Samaritans are free on 116 123 if you need to talk.

How is it different from the Foundation Course?

Module 3 of the Foundation Course introduces calm. iCan Be Calm goes much deeper, focusing completely on understanding, managing and overcoming stress.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. Join iCan Start free and you will get three taster lessons, including Find Your Calm.

How much does it cost?

The course is included with iCan Commit at £19 a month or £190 a year, with a founding member price of £14 a month until 2 January 2027. Weekly drop-ins and one-to-one coaching are also available.
