What we offer

iCan Thinking is coaching and education. It is not therapy, counselling or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling with your mental health, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

Paying and notice

  • Monthly plans continue until you cancel. To cancel, give us one month's notice by email to info@icanthinking.com. You pay for the notice month, and the plan ends when it finishes.
  • iCan Together weekly drop-ins are a six month engagement, with fees paid monthly. After the six months, the plan continues monthly and you can cancel with one month's notice.
  • iCan Mastermind groups are a 12 month engagement, with fees paid monthly.
  • Yearly plans are paid in advance for 12 months.

Leaving early

We want you to get real value from iCan Thinking. If for any reason you do not find full value in the process, you can leave a weekly drop-in, a mastermind group or a yearly plan early. Just tell us by email at info@icanthinking.com.

  • Fees you have paid up to that point are not refunded, but you will not be liable for the rest of the year's fees.
  • If you have paid a full year's fee in advance, you are entitled to a refund of the proportionate fee for the time remaining after your cancellation takes effect. We will pay it promptly, to the payment method you used.

If you are a consumer buying from us online, you have the right to cancel your order within 14 days, starting the day we confirm it, without giving a reason. This right is for individuals buying for personal use. It does not apply if you are buying for your business, and iCan Grow is covered by its own written agreement.
