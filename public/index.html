Questions

Quick answers before you start.

What is iCan Thinking?

iCan Thinking is a positive-thinking method and app created by coach Marcus Thomlinson. It helps you reduce stress, set goals you truly believe in and build daily habits, using See it, Feel it, Believe it.

Is it really free to start?

Yes. iCan Start is free and includes the community, core daily habits, Great Wins and taster lessons. You can step up whenever you're ready.

What is the difference between a course and a weekly drop-in?

A course (iCan Commit) is guided videos and lessons you do at your own pace. The weekly drop-in (iCan Together) adds a live hour every week: 20 minutes of teaching on your course, 20 minutes of live coaching on questions you send in beforehand, and 20 minutes of accountability. It includes everything in Commit.

How long do I commit for?

Weekly drop-ins are a six month engagement, paid monthly, because that is about how long it takes to put the work in. After that it is one month's notice. Business mastermind groups are a 12 month engagement, paid monthly.

How many people are in a mastermind group?

No more than 12, so everyone gets time to ask questions and be heard.

Do I need to download anything?

No. The iCan app runs in any web browser on your phone, tablet or computer at app.icanthinking.com.

How does one-to-one coaching work?

iCan Personal is by application. You'll have weekly sessions for three months, fortnightly for three months, then monthly for six, with a Private Coaching Portal to keep momentum between calls.

What is iCan Grow?

iCan Grow is a business course and programme for owner-managed businesses, with flexible fee deferment available for start-ups and businesses going through a tough patch.
