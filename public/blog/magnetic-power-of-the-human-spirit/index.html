The Magnetic Power of the Human Spirit

Why your inner charge determines your outer life

  • By Marcus Thomlinson
  • Originally published 10 August 2025
  • 6 min read

Imagine a plain piece of steel. Cold. Inactive. Unremarkable. Left to itself, it lies dormant, incapable of lifting even a feather. But pass it through a magnetic field, infuse it with energy and purpose, and suddenly, this once-ordinary material becomes a powerhouse. Now magnetised, it can lift up to 12 times its own weight. And yet, if it loses that charge, it reverts to impotence.

This simple scientific observation is a powerful metaphor for the human condition.

We, too, are made of potential. But potential alone is not power. We must become magnetised, charged with energy, purpose, clarity, and resonance, if we are to become attractors of success, love, wealth, health, and joy. Just as the magnetised steel draws objects into its field, a human being charged with positive energy and alignment draws into their life all that resonates with their frequency.

From Inert to Inspired

Let’s begin with the unmagnetised human, the steel that lies cold and still.

When you are unmotivated, pessimistic, filled with doubt or fear, or have been dulled by stress, defeat, or trauma, your inner charge fades. You may still exist, but you don’t resonate. Your life becomes a reaction to external events instead of a creative response. You might feel like you’re just “going through the motions,” wondering why nothing’s working out.

This is the state of being inert, where your inner power is sleeping. Just like steel that hasn’t been magnetised, you’re capable of so much more, but without the spark of activation, your true strength lies hidden.

Magnetisation: The Activation of Inner Power

What happens when steel is passed through a magnetic field? The molecules within align. Instead of pointing in random directions, they begin to move in unity. That alignment is the key to its power.

Humans work the same way.

When you align your thoughts, emotions, values, actions, and desires toward a common vision, your energy field changes. You become coherent, your inner state becomes unified. This inner congruence generates a frequency, a magnetic field of its own.

In this state, you begin to attract rather than chase. You draw people, ideas, and opportunities toward you. Things begin to “click.” Synchronicities appear. Luck seems to be on your side. But it’s not luck, it’s alignment.

This is the essence of personal magnetism. And it’s not mystical, it’s measurable in your presence, your communication, your energy, your results.

The Science and Spirit of Attraction

Everything in the universe, including you, is vibrating energy. Quantum physics confirms this. And all vibration emits frequency. That frequency interacts with everything around it, resonating with things of similar frequency and repelling those that are out of tune.

This is why you can walk into a room and feel tension, or immediately feel drawn to someone with a bright, calm, confident energy. That’s frequency recognition. That’s magnetism.

Now consider your daily thoughts.

If you constantly think in terms of fear, failure, and frustration, those thoughts charge your field with chaotic, heavy, low-vibrational energy. And what do you attract when your field is heavy and negative? More circumstances, people, and outcomes that match that vibration: stress, conflict, lack, and illness.

But when your thoughts are filled with gratitude, clarity, joy, and purpose, even in the face of challenge, your frequency rises. You become magnetic to high-frequency outcomes.

The Role of Belief and Intention

One of the most powerful tools of magnetisation is belief. Not a passive “I hope this works out,” but an unwavering inner knowing, a certainty in what’s coming. This belief fuels consistent intention. You act not from desperation, but from conviction.

Magnetised people don’t beg the universe, they align with it. Their beliefs are in tune with the outcomes they seek, and their behaviours follow suit. This is why belief work is so crucial in coaching, therapy, and mindset training, it literally rewires your magnetic field.

Discharging Your Power: The Dangers of Negative Energy

Just as magnetised steel can lose its power if exposed to heat or strong opposing fields, humans can be demagnetised too.

  • Chronic stress

  • Toxic environments

  • Negative relationships

  • Doubt, fear, apathy, and bitterness

These are energetic disrupters. They fragment your alignment, scatter your intention, and eventually drain your vitality. Left unchecked, they pull you into a downward spiral, where everything feels harder, heavier, and more hopeless.

This is why healing is so important. It’s not just emotional work, it’s energetic recalibration.

Recharging Your Inner Magnet

So how do you become magnetised? How do you charge yourself with the frequency of what you truly desire?

Here are seven powerful practices:

  1. Clarity of Vision
    Know what you want. Vague goals produce vague results. Create a clear, emotionally resonant picture of your desired life.

  2. Positive Mental Diet
    Monitor your thoughts. Choose uplifting content. Affirm your worth. Delete the mental programmes that no longer serve you.

  3. Daily Alignment Practices
    Whether it’s meditation, breathwork, visualisation, or journaling, make time daily to tune in and charge your frequency.

  4. Gratitude and Joy
    Gratitude is the highest resonance. It makes you magnetic now, not “one day when things get better.” It’s the shortcut to abundance.

  5. Consistent Action
    Move in the direction of your vision, even if it’s a small step. Aligned action supercharges your magnetism.

  6. Elevated Environment
    Surround yourself with people who inspire and uplift you. Energy is contagious, make sure you’re catching the right kind.

  7. Purpose and Service
    When your energy serves others, you amplify your own field. Contribution creates resonance. It gives your magnetism direction.

You Are the Magnet, Not the Metal

The steel doesn’t lift because of what it is, but because of what it has become. Likewise, your value is not static, it’s dynamic. You’re not just flesh and bone; you’re a field of potential. When that field becomes aligned, activated, and positively charged, you become a force.

A magnet doesn’t chase iron filings. It attracts them.

You don’t need to chase success, love, healing, or abundance. You need to become the kind of person that attracts them naturally.

Final Thoughts: Choose Your Charge

Every day, you have a choice:

Will you allow yourself to stay demagnetised, at the mercy of external circumstances?

Or will you rise into conscious magnetism, tuning your frequency to match the life you want to create?

The steel on the workshop floor could lie idle for eternity. But the same steel, magnetised, becomes a lifter of weight, a tool of precision, a force to be reckoned with.

You are no different.

The charge you carry determines the life you attract.

So if life feels heavy, if nothing seems to be moving, check your inner magnet. The power is already within you. You simply have to turn it on.
