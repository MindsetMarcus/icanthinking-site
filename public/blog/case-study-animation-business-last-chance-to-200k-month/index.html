From Ready to Walk Away to a £200k Month: An Animation Business Case Study

A case study in belief, pricing and a final committed attempt

  • A client case study
  • Originally published 11 November 2026
  • 3 min read

Some clients arrive with a plan. This one arrived almost by accident. He had fallen out of love with his animation business, and he was seriously thinking about walking away from something he had once poured his whole heart into.

The animation landscape was shifting. Revenue was down, profit was shrinking, and the spark that had made him start in the first place had gone quiet. Almost on impulse, he went along to a talk on the key mindsets for business. He left revitalised, and he made a decision that changed everything: he would give the business one last, fully committed attempt.

The Starting Point: Falling Out of Love

When we began, turnover was around £80,000 and profitability was going the wrong way. He was a sole proprietor with a small network of subcontractors, and he had lost belief, both in the market and in himself.

His goals were clear and brave. He wanted to reignite his belief in the business and in himself, grow turnover and profit, charge what his work was truly worth, rebrand and reposition, and build a subcontractor model that let him focus on the high-quality work he loved.

The Work: Mindset First, Then Strategy

We started with the iCan Thinking mindsets, and I have to say that I cannot recall a client who got so fully behind the process. He lived and breathed every element of it. Belief was the first thing to return.

Alongside that, we worked through the iCan Grow business strategies in full, with a particular focus on three things:

  • Pricing. Restructuring what he charged so it reflected the real value he delivered.
  • Self-belief and value. Learning to talk about his work with confidence.
  • Brand and story. A new website, a refreshed client story and a market proposition that reconnected with his original vision.

We also built a subcontractor model, so he could spend his time on the work he loved most and the clients who paid best for it.

The Results

By the end of the first year, turnover had risen to just over £200,000, with profitability of around 40%. Then the story kept going. Two and a half years after we first met, he told me he had achieved a £200,000 month for the very first time, something that would have seemed unthinkable at the start.

He now leads a business he genuinely loves, with outstanding clients and a personal transformation in health and wellbeing that is remarkable by any measure.

Results at a Glance

  • Turnover: From around £80,000 to just over £200,000 in the first year.
  • Profitability: Around 40% by the end of year one.
  • Peak month: A £200,000 month, two and a half years later.
  • Business model: Pricing restructured, brand refreshed and a subcontractor network built.
  • Personal: Belief, health and wellbeing transformed.

What This Story Teaches Us

  1. Belief comes before results. When he believed again, the decisions got better.
  2. Commitment is a decision. One honest, fully committed attempt beat years of drifting.
  3. Price reflects belief. Charging what you are worth starts in your thinking, not your spreadsheet.
  4. Your business should serve your life. The right model lets you do the work you love.

The business did not change first. He did, and the business followed.

If you feel a little like he did, tired, unsure and wondering whether to keep going, you might be one decision away from your own turnaround. Have a look at iCan Grow to see how we can work together.
