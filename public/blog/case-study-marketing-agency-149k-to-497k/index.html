From £149k to £497k: A Marketing Agency Case Study

A case study in self-concept, vision and growth

  • A client case study
  • Originally published 9 November 2026
  • 2 min read

Both directors of this marketing agency had come from large agencies, and both were seriously talented. What they had also brought with them was an unconscious belief that they could not command premium fees at a smaller outfit.

That single belief shaped everything. Systems were reactive rather than proactive, internal communication was inconsistent, and a few minor conflicts were simmering under the surface.

The Starting Point

Turnover was £149,000 and profitability was around 18%. The team was two directors, two internal designers and two external designers. Their goals were to set a clear vision and a plan for scaling, increase turnover and profit, grow the team and reduce stress.

The Real Work: Self-Concept

The foundation was self-concept. We helped the directors see the real value they offered, build a compelling vision of the future and create a plan that both of them could fully believe in. At first there was some resistance, and then, once the mindset work took hold, real engagement.

Over 12 months we held around 48 full coaching sessions and 20 check-in sessions. The directors embraced the iCan Thinking mindsets and daily practices, and we implemented the iCan Grow business strategies in full. That brought structure, clarity and direction to a business that had previously operated reactively. New sales strategies brought in a string of high-quality clients, and the team grew by 20%.

The Results

Turnover hit £249,000 after just seven months. The directors were so fired up that they reset their goal to £500,000 at the 12-month mark, and they finished the year at £497,000, with profitability at 40%.

Both directors were calmer, clearer and more energised, and a robust business plan and exit strategy were in place.

Results at a Glance

  • Turnover: From £149,000 to £497,000 in 12 months, more than three times larger.
  • Profitability: From around 18% to 40%.
  • Team: Up by 20%.
  • Direction: A clear plan, a sales strategy and an exit strategy.
  • Wellbeing: Both directors calmer and more energised.

What This Story Teaches Us

  1. Unconscious beliefs set the ceiling. Change the belief and the ceiling lifts.
  2. A shared vision beats a long to-do list. When both directors believed in the plan, everything moved.
  3. Goals can grow. They reset their target mid-year because they had outgrown it.
  4. The real return is a bigger life. One of the directors went on to buy her dream home in France, something she had once believed was not possible for her.

That is the real return on investment.

If you are great at what you do but quietly holding back on what you charge, have a look at iCan Grow.
